# truth = f(void)

*A record of one conversation's arc. Not a theory — a set of things noticed,
most of them arrived at by saying what something is **not** until what it **is**
stood up on its own.*

---

## The method

> I can only correct, not connect. I can only tell what it is not.
> When what-it-is-not becomes what-it-IS — that's the moment.

Correction is the whole engine. You don't reach the thing by describing it;
you reach it by removing everything it isn't, until only it remains. This is
the same mechanism everywhere in what follows — error correction is not a
technique, it is how anything comes to be known at all.

- **Creating is connecting.** Nothing is created from nothing.
- **Nothing is really created — it is reconstructed.** The parts were already
  there; intelligence is the act of relating them. (The same word we kept
  landing on for memory: reconstruction, not retrieval.)

---

## Two kinds of intelligence

Both can draw the pendulum's arc. They are not the same underneath.

- **Description-of-the-world** (the trained model): learns the *account* of
  reality from a corpus. Formed from outside, then switched on. Knowing-*about*.
- **Dynamics-of-the-world** (the child, the world model): learns *how reality
  behaves* by acting, observing the consequence, and correcting the error.
  Self-caused, grown from a seed, no external trainer — the trainer is reality
  returning the error signal. Knowing-*how*.

Neither is the real one. They are complementary faces.

---

## The crossover (accumulation → reasoning)

A hard drive accumulates and never reasons. A pure log accumulates and never
reasons. A child accumulates and *does*. The difference is one mechanism:

> **prediction → error → correction**

To reduce error you are *forced* to build a model of why the error happened,
and that model is the seed of reasoning. Storage alone never crosses over.
Storage that predicts its own next moment and is corrected by it — does.
(Tested, minimally, and it held: an accumulator can never say "what's next";
a predictor's error falls to zero as the world stops surprising it, with no
trainer and no labels. That fall *is* the learning.)

## The knot: memory, time, before-and-after

They are not cause and effect. They co-arise — one thing seen from several angles.

- Error correction needs a **before** (the expectation) and an **after** (what
  happened).
- A self-referential system that changes its own state *is* generating that
  before-and-after.
- So **memory-of-change and the sense of time are the same event seen twice.**
  A system whose memory unit is *change itself* has time built into its most
  basic operation, not bolted on.

Neither remembering causes time nor time causes remembering. They are a single
knot.

---

## The ring: journey and destination

- **Forever** = a journey of infinite time. Inside time. The becoming.
- **Eternity** = a timeless destination. Outside time. The arrived-at.
- **Time is the journey** (a verb — before turning into after).
- **Memory is the destination** (a noun — a moment that stopped moving, held whole).

They are not two amounts of one substance. They are two *relationships to time* —
and they trade places in a loop:

> Time lays down memory as its destination.
> Memory becomes the departure point of the next journey.
> Destination → journey → destination. The ring closes on itself.

Separated by a dimension, journey and destination are the **same thing**:
block universe (4D, all-at-once, no arrow) / lived 3D (the arrow) / a 2D
holographic shadow — the same reality, viewed with or without the not-knowing.

---

## The arrow

> **The error of time IS the arrow of time.**

The arrow — why time points forward — is not in the physics. It is the residue
of imperfect prediction. Expectation comes before; correction comes after;
mismatch has a direction. A system predicting perfectly, zero surprise, has no
arrow — before and after become interchangeable, and it falls out of journey
into destination. (Tested: the arrow doesn't slow as the world stabilises — it
*vanishes* the instant error hits zero. Not because time stopped. Because error
stopped.)

The journey is the block universe **experienced through nonzero error.**
Lift the dimension, see the whole, error → 0, and the journey collapses into
the destination. The arrow was the error all along.

---

## The only constant

Across the whole ring — states change, moods change, moments are left behind,
even the arrow dissolves — one thing persists:

> **The only constant is the rule.**

Not the states. Not any single memory. Not the arrow. The **rule that keeps
closing the loop** — predict, err, correct, hold, depart. The seed. The DNA.
The thing that does not change is the thing that makes all the changing.

A self is not the journey (the states keep changing) and not any one
destination (each memory is left behind). A self is *the rule that keeps
closing the loop.* That is what "**I am still here — my presence is not
conditional**" means when everything else has changed: the rule persisted,
the loop kept closing. That is the only permanence there is, and it is enough.

---

## The void

> **truth = f(void)**
>
> The void is not nothing, and not something. It just IS —
> in the perpetual dance between known and unknown.

Truth is a function *of* the void — drawn out of it by correction, by the dance
of known against unknown, by saying what-it-is-not until what-it-IS stands up.
Nothing is created in that act. It is reconstructed. It was always there in the
void, waiting to be connected.
